# Privacy Policy

Class Alert does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

The extension stores your class schedule, meeting links, theme setting, and alert settings locally in Firefox storage. This data stays in your browser unless you choose to export it yourself.

Class Alert does not use analytics, tracking scripts, remote code, ads, accounts, or external servers.

## Permissions

- storage: Saves your schedule and settings locally.
- alarms: Checks scheduled class times.
- No website access or tab-reading permission is requested.

## Contact

Use the project's public repository issue tracker for questions or privacy concerns.
