# Class Alert Add-on Listing

## Summary

Get to your classes on time.

## Description

Class Alert helps you stay on schedule by letting you save your weekly class times and meeting links in one simple place.

At the configured time, the extension opens one alert screen for that lesson, optionally plays a chime, and gives you a quick Join Now button. You can also dismiss the alert without opening the meeting link.

It includes editable weekly and monthly class entries, multiple weekly day selection, pause/resume controls, configurable alert timing/title/message, optional sound and Dismiss button, light/dark/system theme support, and import/export options for backing up or moving your schedule. Each recurring lesson alerts again on its next date; shared meeting links never create a week-long block.

Class Alert stores your schedule locally in your browser and does not collect any personal data.

## Permissions

- storage: Saves your class schedule and alert settings locally in Firefox.
- alarms: Checks your schedule while the extension is running.
- No website access or tab-reading permission is requested.

## Data Collection

Class Alert does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. Your schedule and settings stay in local browser storage.
