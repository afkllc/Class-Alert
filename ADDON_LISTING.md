# Class Alert Add-on Listing

## Summary

Get to your classes on time.

## Description

Class Alert helps you stay on schedule by letting you save your weekly class times and meeting links in one simple place.

When a class starts, the extension opens an alert screen, plays a chime, and gives you a quick "Join Now" button so you can jump straight into your session.

It includes editable class entries, optional alert sound, smart skipping when the class link is already open in a browser tab, light/dark/system theme support, and import/export options for backing up or moving your schedule.

Class Alert stores your schedule locally in your browser and **does not collect any personal data**.

## Permissions

- `storage`: Saves your class schedule and alert settings locally in Firefox.
- `alarms`: Checks your schedule while the extension is running.
- `tabs`: Opens the alert page and class links, and checks whether a saved class link is already open.

## Data Collection

Class Alert does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. Your schedule and settings stay in local browser storage.
